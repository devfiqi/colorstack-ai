# ColorStack AI

Your personal AI assistant for handling ColorStack tasks.

Currently Inactive

## Overview

ColorStack AI turns activity from the University of Minnesota ColorStack
Discord into a clear picture of what needs attention. It helps chapter leaders
stay on top of events, responsibilities, follow-ups, and important updates from
one private workspace.

## Motivation

Important chapter work is often spread across channels, threads, meeting notes,
and individual conversations. That makes it easy to miss a deadline, lose an
action item, or spend too much time piecing together what is happening.

ColorStack AI organizes that information into something useful so leaders can
spend less time searching and more time moving the chapter forward.

## What it does

- See what needs attention today
- Keep track of events and their requirements
- Find action items and follow-ups from Discord
- Ask questions using relevant ColorStack context
- Review a daily brief of important updates
- Add notes or conversations for the assistant to organize
- Surface blockers, missing work, and suggested next steps

## Built for trust

ColorStack AI reads approved ColorStack information, organizes it, and presents
useful summaries in a private dashboard. Suggested facts from pasted notes must
be approved before the assistant uses them.

The assistant is advisory only. It does not take action, change external
systems, or send anything to Discord unless a person explicitly tells it to.
